Privacy policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

This policy covers Max Quattromani's personal website at maxquattromani.com.

Visiting the site

The current site does not use site analytics, advertising trackers, or custom cookies or browser storage. You can browse without creating an account or submitting a form.

GitHub Pages hosts this site. GitHub logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, even when visitors are not signed in to GitHub. See GitHub's explanation of GitHub Pages and GitHub's privacy statement for information about its handling of personal data.

Emailing me

The contact link opens your email application. If you send a message, I receive your email address, your message, and any other information you choose to include. I use that information to read and respond to your inquiry. Email services also process and store messages as part of delivering them.

Please avoid sending sensitive personal information. You can email {{ site.email }} with a privacy question or a request to delete correspondence. I can explain what information I hold and what I can remove; I cannot control records held independently by service providers.

External resources and links

Some historical examples load images or audio from other websites. Loading those resources sends a request to the provider, which can receive your IP address and other request information. Following an external link takes you to a separate website. Those providers' privacy practices apply to their services.

Changes to this policy

I will update this policy if the site's services or practices change. The date above identifies the latest update.
