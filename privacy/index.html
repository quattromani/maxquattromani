Privacy policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

This policy covers Max Quattromani's personal website at maxquattromani.com.

Browsing this site

This site does not use analytics or advertising trackers. Its code does not set cookies or save choices in browser storage. You can read its pages without an account. There is no contact form that sends information to a server.

GitHub Pages hosts the site. GitHub logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, including when visitors are not signed in to GitHub. See GitHub Pages data collection and GitHub's privacy statement for its practices.

Contact options and email

The Contact page offers an email link and an optional topic selector. Choosing a topic only changes the suggested email subject in your browser. The email link asks your configured email application to start a draft; you decide whether to send it. The site does not send the message.

If you use the copy button, the site writes the email address to your clipboard. It does not read your clipboard or save your topic selection.

If you send an email, I receive your address, message, and any attachments you include, and use them to read and respond to your inquiry. Email providers process and store messages as part of delivering them. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information.

For a privacy question or a request about correspondence you have sent, email {{ site.email }}. Describe your request without including sensitive documents. I can explain what correspondence I hold and discuss what can be removed. Records held independently by service providers are outside my control.

External resources and links

Some historical technical examples load images from other websites when you open the page. Those requests can share your IP address and browser request information with the image provider. External links take you to separate websites. Each provider's privacy practices apply to its services.

County business

This personal website is separate from the county's services. For assessment questions, official records, or county business, use the Gage County Assessor's official website and its contact options.

Policy updates

Changes to the site's services or practices may require an update to this policy. The date above identifies the latest revision.
