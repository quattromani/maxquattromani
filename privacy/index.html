Privacy policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This policy covers Max Quattromani's personal website at maxquattromani.com.

Browsing this site

This site does not use analytics or advertising trackers. Its code does not set cookies or save choices in browser storage. You can read its pages without an account.

GitHub Pages hosts the site. GitHub logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, including when visitors are not signed in to GitHub. See GitHub Pages data collection and GitHub's privacy statement for its practices.

Contact form

{% if site.data.contact.enabled %}

When you send the contact form, your name, email address, selected topic, and message go to Formspree, the service that handles submissions for this site. Formspree processes the submission, stores it as part of its service, and sends an email notification to me. I use your message to read and respond to your inquiry.

Formspree can also collect request information, such as your IP address and browser details, to operate the service and filter spam. Its handling and retention of submissions depend on its service policies and account settings. See Formspree's privacy policy.

It does not save a draft in browser storage. Please do not include sensitive personal information; the form does not accept attachments.

Email and the copy button

The email link asks your configured email application to start a draft; you decide whether to send it. If you use the copy button, the site writes the email address to your clipboard. It does not read your clipboard.

If you send an email, I receive your address, message, and any attachments you include, and use them to read and respond to your inquiry. Email providers process and store messages as part of delivering them. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information.

For a privacy question or a request about correspondence you have sent, email {{ site.email }}. Describe your request without including sensitive documents. I can explain what correspondence I hold and discuss what can be removed. Records held independently by service providers are outside my control.

External resources and links

Some historical technical examples load images from other websites when you open the page. Those requests can share your IP address and browser request information with the image provider. External links take you to separate websites. Each provider's privacy practices apply to its services.
