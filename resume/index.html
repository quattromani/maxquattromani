--- title: Career and selected work description: "Selected roles and projects from Max Quattromani’s career." format: markdown presentation: current --- My work has spanned web development, design, technical leadership, consulting, and business operations. These are selected roles and projects from that history. ## Independent business and consulting **Parsimony Shoppes | Founder and business operator | From 2019** I founded Parsimony Shoppes, an online resale business. My work included building its storefront, developing inventory and storage processes, preparing product listings, and managing day-to-day operations. **Qoppa | Principal | 2017 to 2022** Through Qoppa, I provided design and development consulting, including web development, brand identity, packaging, and marketing materials. **Placewise Digital | Contract consulting | 2017 to 2020** After my full-time role, I continued consulting on website migrations, front-end architecture, and reusable site templates. ## Development leadership **Placewise Media | Senior director, development | 2014 to 2017** I led a development team working across front-end, back-end, and data disciplines. We developed modular site architecture and a static site generator that incorporated data from application programming interfaces. I also coordinated product decisions with executive, marketing, and sales teams. **GLG | Software engineer | 2014** I worked on front-end components, mobile application design, and a style guide and pattern library for internal applications. **Time Warner Cable | Front-end development manager | 2013 to 2014** I developed a responsive framework for the company's website and online commerce, brought front-end responsibilities in-house from a third-party vendor, and documented development practices for the team. I also created a component-prototyping system to help stakeholders review design ideas. **Time Warner Cable | Senior web design and development consultant | 2012 to 2013** I contributed to the redesign of the customer-facing website, working with design and development teams on responsive layouts, forms, and browser consistency. ## Earlier work **OraLabs | Creative director | 2012** I led work across web development, product and packaging design, email marketing, catalogs, and other marketing materials. **Placewise Media | Front-end UX/UI engineer | 2011 to 2012** I developed websites for shopping centers and commercial properties, contributing to shared interfaces and consistent behavior across browsers. **OraLabs | Web developer | 2010 to 2011** I worked on corporate and product websites, email marketing, and packaging design, and helped colleagues with databases, spreadsheets, and reporting. ## Selected project: Scaffold I created Scaffold in 2014 to bring reusable components, a style guide, and development practices into one framework. It grew from a collection of tools I had been using in my own work and became the project I took to my GLG interview. The project reflects a recurring interest in my career: making decisions once, documenting them, and giving teams a consistent foundation they can adapt. [View Scaffold on GitHub](https://github.com/quattromani/scaffold).
