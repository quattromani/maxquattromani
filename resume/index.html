--- title: Career and selected work description: "An account of Max Quattromani’s work in development, team leadership, consulting, and independent business." format: markdown presentation: current --- Web development has been my work since {{ site.data.profile.career_start }}. Over time, building individual websites became work on shared systems, development teams, and the daily operations of a business. The projects below show how those responsibilities developed. ## Websites, products, and design At OraLabs, web development was closely connected to the products the company made. As a web developer from 2010 to 2011, I worked on corporate and product websites, email campaigns, and packaging, while helping colleagues with databases, spreadsheets, and reporting. A move to Placewise Media in 2011 brought a different challenge: developing websites for shopping centers and commercial properties, with shared interfaces that behaved consistently across browsers. After that role ended in 2012, I returned to OraLabs as creative director, leading work on websites, packaging, catalogs, and marketing materials. ## Bringing development in-house In 2012, I joined Time Warner Cable as a senior web design and development consultant, becoming front-end development manager in 2013 and serving in that role until 2014. The initial work supported the redesign of its customer-facing website, including responsive layouts, forms, and browser consistency. As manager, I developed a responsive framework for the website and online commerce and brought front-end responsibilities in-house from an outside vendor. A component-prototyping system gave stakeholders a way to review design ideas during development. Documented practices in the team’s wiki provided a reference for the people building and maintaining the site. ## Scaffold and work at GLG Preparing for a GLG interview in 2014 gave me a reason to bring my collection of code snippets, components, and development practices into one project. The result was [Scaffold](https://github.com/quattromani/scaffold), a framework with reusable components and a style guide that I could use in my own work and share with other developers. That same year, as a software engineer at GLG, my work included front-end components, mobile application design, and a style guide and pattern library for internal applications. ## Leading a development team Returning to Placewise Media as senior director of development from 2014 to 2017 meant responsibility for a team working across front-end, back-end, and data disciplines. Together, we developed modular site architecture and a static site generator that incorporated data from other systems. The shared architecture gave the team a common foundation for building commercial property websites. The role also required coordination with executive, marketing, and sales teams on product decisions. After leaving the full-time position, I continued consulting for Placewise Digital from 2017 to 2020 on website migrations, front-end architecture, and reusable site templates. ## Independent consulting and Parsimony Shoppes Through Qoppa, where I was principal from 2017 to 2022, the work extended from web development to brand identity, packaging, and marketing materials. In 2019, I founded Parsimony Shoppes, an online resale business. Building the storefront was one part of running it. Inventory needed product identifiers, storage locations, barcodes, and listings, so I developed processes connecting the digital catalog to the items on the shelves. The work also included product presentation and day-to-day operations. {{ site.data.profile.recent_work }} ## Preparing for public service Since 2025, much of my time has gone toward certification study and continuing education in assessment. {{ site.data.profile.certification }} My [About page](/about/) describes the completed coursework and study still underway as I prepare for an anticipated transition into the Gage County Assessor’s office, subject to the election result.
