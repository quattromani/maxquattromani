Accessibility

Last updated: October 13, 2026

I want this site to be usable by people with disabilities. My goal is to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA.

Current status

The rebuilt site includes a skip link, page landmarks, keyboard controls for navigation, and native dialogs. Initial checks in Chrome covered navigation, dialog closing and focus behavior, and a narrow mobile layout.

A full accessibility audit has not been completed, and I am not claiming WCAG conformance. Older PDFs, historical examples, and embedded media have not all been reviewed. They may present accessibility barriers. A broader review, including assistive technology testing, is still needed.

Report a barrier

If you have trouble using a page or accessing its information, email {{ site.email }}. Please include the page URL and describe the problem or the information you need. If helpful, you can also include your browser, device, or assistive technology. You do not need to share a diagnosis or other sensitive personal information.

You can also use that address to request the information in another format. I will review the issue and discuss an alternative with you where possible.
