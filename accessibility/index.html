Accessibility

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Max Quattromani maintains this personal website. My goal is for people with disabilities to be able to read its content and use its navigation and contact options. The target is the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA.

Current status and limitations

The site includes a skip link, page landmarks, and keyboard controls for the mobile navigation. A full accessibility evaluation, including manual and assistive technology testing, is still pending. This statement does not claim WCAG conformance.

Historical writing and technical examples have not all been reviewed. Images may lack useful text alternatives, and older examples may be difficult to use with assistive technology. If a page prevents you from getting the information you need, please report it or request another format.

The contact form continues to Formspree for spam verification and confirmation. If that verification presents a barrier, you can email me directly using the address below instead.

Get help or report a barrier

Email {{ site.email }} with the page URL and a description of the problem or the information you need. You can use the same address to request another format. If the email link does not open your email application, copy the address into a new message.

Browser, device, or assistive technology details can help explain a problem, but they are optional. You do not need to provide a diagnosis or medical information. I will review the request and discuss an alternative where possible.

For help with county assessment services, use the Gage County Assessor's official website and its contact options.
